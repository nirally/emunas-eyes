# PLAYTEST-5k — touch to interact (~5 min)

You can now use things by touching them: when the game shows its interact prompt, press a hand against the door, button, panel or Gridnode in front of you instead of pressing A. Keep the hand there for things you normally hold A for. A still works as before.

Start the game as usual, get into gameplay, then:
- find something with an interact prompt (a door, a button, a Gridnode, a collectible) and push a hand into it
- for a hold-type one, keep the hand pressed until it finishes
- play normally for a couple of minutes (run, wall-run, climb, touch walls along the way)
then quit normally.

1. Did touching work on the things you tried (which ones)?
2. Did a hold-type interaction finish while you kept the hand there?
3. Anything triggered that you didn't mean to, e.g. while wall-running or climbing?
4. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes door and button, 2 yes, 3 no, 4 5".
