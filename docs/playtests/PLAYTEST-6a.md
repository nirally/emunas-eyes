# PLAYTEST-6a — physical punching (~10 min)

Punch with your fists: a fast punch in front of you (either hand, straight or a hook) makes Faith attack (her light attack). You feel a short buzz when a punch counts. The X / Y buttons still work as before. Slow reaches, doors and buttons work as before (a fast flick into a door still slams it).

Start the game, get to a fight (KrugerSec patrol or a combat mission), then:
- punch enemies: single jabs, then quick combos with both hands, then hooks
- also punch the air with no enemy around
- run, climb and wall-run normally for a minute, moving your hands like you usually do
- slam a door with a fast flick, open one with a slow reach
then quit normally.

1. Do your punches turn into Faith's attacks reliably? Any missed, or late?
2. Any attacks you didn't mean to throw (while climbing, running, reaching)?
3. Speed: do you have to punch too hard, or does it fire too easily?
4. Doors: slow reach and fast flick still right? And if you punch a door (fist stops before it), does it open?
5. Hooks / strong swings: should they stay light attacks, or become Faith's heavy kick? (e.g. "hook = heavy")
6. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 no, 3 fine, 4 yes / yes, 5 hook = heavy, 6 5".
