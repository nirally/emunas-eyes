# PLAYTEST-8a — HUD and menus pulled in from the edges (~5 min)

New: the game draws a wider picture than your headset shows, so its corners (the health bar, the pause menu) were cut off. Now all of the game's HUD and menus are drawn smaller, in the middle of your view. The setting is ui.scale (0.4 now; bigger = larger but closer to the edges).

Play a minute (look for the health bar bottom left), get hit once if you can, then open the pause menu, Options, and the map. If an objective marker shows, look at whether it sits on the place it points to. Quit normally.

1. Can you see the whole health bar now, bottom left? (yes / partly / no)
2. Is it a good size and place? (yes / too small / too big / too far down or left)
3. Pause menu and Options: can you see all of it? Can you read the text? (all + readable / all but text too small / still cut off)
4. The map: usable? (yes / too small / cut off)
5. Objective markers or prompts: on the thing they point at? (yes / off to the side / didn't see any)
6. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 all + readable, 4 yes, 5 yes, 6 5".
