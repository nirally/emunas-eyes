# PLAYTEST-5m — Stage 5 gate: one mission with your hands (~20-30 min)

Nothing new to learn: play one full mission (main or side) as you normally would, using your hands for doors, buttons and Gridnodes (slow reach = use, fast flick = slam). If you pass a collectible, grab it. Quit normally afterwards.

1. Over the whole mission, did the hands stay right (on your hands, stopping at walls, fingers following)?
2. Did touch / flick work every time, or did you fall back to A? If so, where?
3. Collectibles: did picking them up work?
4. Anything wrong or annoying, even small?
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 always worked, 3 yes, 4 nothing, 5 5".
