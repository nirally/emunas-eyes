# PLAYTEST-2b — full field of view (~10 min)

Before: Anti-aliasing off (or FXAA), Motion blur off (as in 2a). Starts in 3D with the full field of view.
Keys: F9 full field of view on/off · F4 3D on/off · F6 recentre · F3/F2 height.

1. Look around. Does the picture fill the headset now, or is there still a black border? Where?
2. Press F9 a few times to compare full view with the old "window". Which do you prefer?
3. Sharpness: how blurry is the full view (1 = unreadable, 5 = as sharp as before)? Read a distant sign.
4. Edges: anything stretched, bent or wrong near the edges of your view? Straight lines bending while you turn your head?
5. Size: do doors and people still look the right size?
6. Smoothness: any stutter or lower frame rate compared with before?
7. Comfort 1-5: nausea / eye strain.

Reply example: "1 full, 2 prefer full, 3 sharpness 3, 4 nothing, 5 right, 6 smooth, 7 1/1".
