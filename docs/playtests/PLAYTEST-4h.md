# PLAYTEST-4h — Stage 4 sign-off: a full mission (~20-30 min)

Why: this closes Stage 4 (movement and comfort). Play one whole story mission (or a long free-roam run) the way you'd normally play, using everything: running, wall-runs, climbing, sliding by crouching, real steps, turning, fights, recentre when needed. After it, Stage 5 starts: your hands in the game.

Afterwards:
1. Over the whole mission, did anything about moving feel wrong or uncomfortable? (name it)
2. Any moment where the view did something on its own (jump, drift, spin)?
3. Did you use the recentre? Did room-scale ever get in the way (Faith walking when you didn't want)?
4. Cutscene or takedown seen? Anything wrong? (carried since Stage 1)
5. Comfort over the whole session, 5 = very comfortable.
