# PLAYTEST-3a — play with the Quest 2 controllers (~15 min)

Why: the Touch controllers now act as the game's Xbox controller. Put the keyboard and mouse aside and play with the controllers only.
Note: if you also have an Xbox controller or Steam Input running, the Touch controllers take over controller 1 while the headset is on.

Controls (the game's own controller layout):
- Left stick: move. Right stick left/right: turn. (Look up/down with your head.)
- Left grip: jump / climb / wall-run (Up actions). Left trigger: crouch / slide / roll (Down actions).
- Right trigger: Shift (dodge). Right grip: quick turn.
- X: light attack. Y: heavy attack. A: interact. B: Disruptor.
- Right stick click: Runner's Vision. Left stick click: map. Left menu button (≡): pause; the sticks and A/B work in the menus.
Turn speed = the game's Options > Controls > Controller sensitivity.

In the headset:
1. Can you do everything with the controllers? Anything that doesn't work or feels stuck? (name it)
2. Do the menus work (pause, options, map)?
3. Do you feel vibration when you land hard, get hit or hit someone?
4. Turning with the right stick: comfortable? Speed OK? (or "faster" / "slower")
5. Any button you'd rather have somewhere else? (e.g. "jump on A")
6. Carried: cutscene or takedown seen? Anything wrong? (or "none")
7. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 all works, 2 yes, 3 yes, 4 ok, 5 jump on A, 6 none, 7 5".
