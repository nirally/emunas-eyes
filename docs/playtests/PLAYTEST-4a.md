# PLAYTEST-4a — forward is where you look, new turning, real crouch (~15 min)

What changed:
- Forward is now where you look. Faith's body turns with your head, so push the stick forward and she runs where you face. Wall-runs and jumps go where you look.
- Turning with the right stick is now done by the mod: smooth, 180 degrees per second at full stick (the game's Controller sensitivity no longer matters for turning). Snap turn is available (ask if you want it).
- Crouch for real (head about 30 cm lower than standing) and Faith crouches; crouch while running and she slides.
- Quick turn (right grip) and the game's own turns still turn your view, as before.

In the headset:
1. Look left/right and push the stick forward: does Faith run where you look? (yes/no)
2. Right-stick turning at the new speed: OK / faster / slower?
3. Real crouch: does Faith crouch, and slide when running? Any accidental crouching when you didn't mean to?
4. Wall-runs, jumps, quick turn: anything feel wrong, jerky or fighting you?
5. Any spinning, drifting or view jumping on its own?
6. Carried: cutscene or takedown seen?
7. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 ok, 3 yes no, 4 fine, 5 no, 6 none, 7 5".
