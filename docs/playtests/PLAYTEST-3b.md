# PLAYTEST-3b — pause, map, vibration, turn speed (~10 min)

Changes since 3a:
- Pause: tap the LEFT STICK CLICK (press the left stick in). Map: hold it in for half a second.
- Vibration: fixed a likely cause; the log now records it either way.
- Turn speed: the game's Controller sensitivity is at the middle. Pause (tap left stick) -> Options -> Controls -> Controller sensitivity: push it right (use the left stick to move, A to select, B to go back).

2. Do you feel vibration now (hard landings, getting hit, hitting someone)?
3. With Controller sensitivity raised: turning fast enough now? (or "still slow at max")
4. Anything else?
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.
