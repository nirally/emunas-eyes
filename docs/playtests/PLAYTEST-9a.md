# PLAYTEST-9a: the settings menu in the headset (~10 min)

New: an in-VR settings menu with every mod setting in it.
- Open / close: HOLD the left menu button (the one with three lines) for 1 second. A short TAP still pauses the game as before.
- Either stick up/down: move. Left/right: change the value. A: open a section / pick. B: back (B on the first page closes it).
- Changes apply at once and are saved. While the menu is open Faith stands still.

1. Hold the menu button: does the menu appear in front of you, and is the text easy to read? (too small / fine / too big)
2. Open Comfort and change "Extra eye height" a few steps up and back down: does your view move at once?
3. Open Hands and turn "Show the extra gloved hands" On and back Off: do the gloves appear and go?
4. Does a short tap of the menu button still pause the game?
5. Anything awkward: moving through the list, the stick repeating too fast or too slow, the menu in the wrong place?
6. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 fine, 2 yes, 3 yes, 4 yes, 5 no, 6 5".
