# PLAYTEST-9c: profiles (~5 min)

New: a Profiles page in the settings menu - 4 saved copies of your settings, so someone else can use the headset and you switch back with one pick.
- Open the menu: click both sticks in and hold 1 s. Profiles is near the bottom of the first page.

1. In Calibration, change "Extra eye height" a few steps up and back down: does your view move at once? (9b's question 2 - it's under Calibration, not Comfort)
2. Open Profiles and pick "Save your settings as profile 1" (A). Does Load profile 1 now show the date and time?
3. Go to Hands and turn "Show the extra gloved hands" On. Then Profiles > Load profile 1, A, A again: do the gloves go away?
4. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 yes, 4 5".
