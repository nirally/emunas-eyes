# PLAYTEST-5a — do the hand markers sit on your controllers? (~5 min)

New: a small box with a stick shows where each controller is (left blue, right orange). They are placeholders for the real hands (next step); right now we only check they're in exactly the right place.

1. Hold a controller in front of your face and look at it: is the box where the controller's handle is? If not: how far off and which way (e.g. "5 cm too high", "too far forward")?
2. Does the stick point the way the controller points (forward out of the ring)? If not, which way is it off?
3. Move your hands fast: do the boxes stay on them or lag/float behind?
4. Both boxes show in both eyes, no flicker? Hidden correctly when you put a hand "into" a wall?
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 stay, 4 yes yes, 5 5".
