# PLAYTEST-10a: left-handed, then seated (~15 min)

Stage 10 checks that everything also works left-handed and sitting down. Stop at once if you feel warm, sweaty or dizzy.

## Part 0 - Faith's left arm (BUG-037, 2 min, normal right-handed)
In free roam, raise both arms and turn your wrists (palms up, palms down, reach up like in your screenshot). Hold Y + B once with her bare left arm in view.

0. Her left elbow and forearm: still twisted or creased, or smooth now?

## Part 1 - left-handed (standing)
Settings menu (both sticks, hold 1 s) > Controls > "Left-handed" On. The layout mirrors at once:
- RIGHT hand: stick = move, grip = Up actions (jump), trigger = Down actions (slide), A = light attack, B = heavy attack, stick click = tap pause / hold map.
- LEFT hand: stick = turn, grip = quick turn, trigger = Shift, X = interact, Y = Disruptor, stick click = tap Runner Vision / hold recentre.

1. Running, jumping, sliding, wall-runs, climbing: do they work like right-handed, just mirrored?
2. Turning with the left stick, and recentre (hold the left stick click 1 s): do they work?
3. Punch with each hand, an open-hand push (Disruptor), a MAG Rope throw: do they still work?
4. Faith's arms: does her left arm stay on your left controller, right on your right?
5. Anything that still feels right-handed?

Turn "Left-handed" back Off.

## Part 2 - seated
Sit down, look straight ahead, hold the right stick click 1 s (recentre). Then Calibration > "Extra eye height" until Faith's view height feels right.

6. What eye height did you end up with (the number in the menu)?
7. Play 5 minutes sitting (run, jump, a fight if one is near): does she crouch or slide by herself when you lean forward or reach down?
8. Can you reach well enough for punches and the MAG Rope throw?
9. Profiles > "Save your settings as profile 2" (your seated setup). Then load profile 1 again (your standing one) and stand up.
10. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable (one number for each part if they differ).

Reply example: "0 smooth, 1 yes, 2 yes, 3 yes, 4 yes, 5 no, 6 0.40, 7 no, 8 yes, 9 done, 10 5".
