# PLAYTEST-8m: sound, button prompts, walls (~5 min)

Nothing new to install; this answers three questions for the rest of Stage 8.

1. Sound: stand still near something that makes a sound from one place (a guard talking, a drone, a radio, a vent). Turn only your head left and right, not the stick. Does the sound move between your ears as your head turns, or does it stay where it was?
2. Prompts: the game shows Xbox buttons. A/B/X/Y are the same letters on your Touch controllers; LB/RB are your grips and LT/RT your triggers. Is that fine, or do you want the prompts to show grip/trigger?
3. Inside a building, put a hand flat on a wall: does it stop at the wall or go through?
4. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 stays, 2 fine, 3 stops, 4 5".
