# PLAYTEST-6h — MAG Rope and Disruptor by hand (~10 min)

New: **MAG Rope = throw.** When a MAG Rope point shows, throw either hand up toward it (fast, ending above your chin). The mod holds the grip button for you while your arm stays up; lower the arm below your shoulder to let go. **Disruptor = open-hand push**: take every finger off the controller (trigger finger, thumb, grip loose) and push your palm toward the camera/turret, like a punch. The buttons still work.

Find a few MAG Rope points (swing and pull-up if you have them) and a Disruptor target if your save has one. Also punch normally once or twice in a fight. Quit normally.

1. MAG Rope by throwing: did it fire when you threw? Did lowering the arm let go at the right time?
2. Anything stray: unwanted jumps when throwing with no point nearby, or throws when you meant to punch?
3. Disruptor push: did it work? Any Disruptor when you meant to punch?
4. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes yes, 2 no, 3 yes no, 4 5" (or "3 n/a" if there was no Disruptor target).
