# PLAYTEST-9b: the settings menu, take two (~10 min)

New: the menu button didn't work because SteamVR keeps it for its own dashboard. The menu now opens differently. Faith's arms are also learned again when her outfit changes (the jacket after aborting the first mission).
- Open / close: CLICK BOTH STICKS IN and hold them 1 second. Releasing them pauses nothing and opens nothing else.
- Either stick up/down: move. Left/right: change the value. A: open a section / pick. B: back (B on the first page closes it).
- Changes apply at once and are saved. While the menu is open Faith stands still.

1. Hold both sticks: does the menu appear in front of you, and is the text easy to read? (too small / fine / too big)
2. Open Comfort and change "Extra eye height" a few steps up and back down: does your view move at once?
3. Open Hands and turn "Show the extra gloved hands" On and back Off: do the gloves appear and go?
4. Do a short tap on the left stick (pause) and on the right stick (Runner Vision) still work as before?
5. Anything awkward: moving through the list, the stick repeating too fast or too slow, the menu in the wrong place?
6. Do Faith's hands follow the controllers in free roam? If you start a mission where her outfit changes, do they come back within a few seconds?
7. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.
