# PLAYTEST-7l — a real fight (~5 min)

7k was punches in the air only (no enemies). Same build plus one new log line: how far her chest is from the direction of your hands, during attacks and between them.

Find enemies and fight them: straight punches, hooks, a guard, with each hand, and a kick or two. Look down at her body and arms while you do. Quit normally.

1. Does her body / shoulders swing away from your arms when you hit someone? (no / sometimes / often)
2. Do her arms look right with her body in the fight? (yes / mostly / no)
3. Do your hands ever get stopped by her body? (no / yes, when ...)
4. Anything else broken? (e.g. her body snapping when a fight move ends, a takedown or getting hit looking wrong)
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 no, 2 yes, 3 no, 4 no, 5 5".
