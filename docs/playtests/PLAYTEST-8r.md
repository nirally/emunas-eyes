# PLAYTEST-8r: Stage 8 check - the game's UI in the headset (~15 min)

Nothing new to install. This is the last check before Stage 8 passes: a few things nobody has looked at in the headset yet. Play on from where you are (the gridLink walk, then the mission). Hold Y + B for a screenshot whenever something looks off.

1. Runner Vision: when objects on your route glow red, do they look like part of the world (at the right distance, one clear image), not floating or doubled?
2. If the game shows you the route (the red ghost runner / trail), same question.
3. Objective marker (the waypoint icon on your goal): does it sit on its target, or is it off towards the middle of your view?
4. Subtitles: can you read them comfortably? (too small / fine / too big)
5. In a fight, if you see them: enemy stamina bars and the marker over enemies - on the enemies, or off?
6. The health bar, prompts, pause menu and map: still fine?
7. Anything in the UI that's hard to see, doubled, or in the wrong place?
8. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 off to the middle, 4 too small, 5 didn't see, 6 yes, 7 no, 8 5".
