# PLAYTEST-10c: the opening hour, end to end (2-3 sessions)

Play the game's opening from the start of the first mission onwards, as a player would, for up to an hour in total (several sessions are fine). Stop at once if you feel warm, sweaty or dizzy, and take a break.

Please note anything that breaks the game or feels wrong; hold Y + B the moment something looks off (it saves a headset screenshot and marks the log).

1. Did you get through the first mission (and on, if you kept going)? Where did you stop?
2. Faith's hands and arms (free roam, jacket, wall-runs, ledges, fights): anything odd?
3. Cutscenes, menus, the map, subtitles: readable and usable?
4. Fights (punches, kicks, Disruptor push): did they work as you expected?
5. F5 (feel the game's camera motion) on your keyboard: does it turn on and off without turning the 3D off (BUG-032)?
6. Anything that made you stop, restart or take the headset off?
7. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable (one per session if they differ).

Reply example: "1 yes, stopped at the second mission, 2 no, 3 yes, 4 yes, 5 yes, 6 no, 7 5".
