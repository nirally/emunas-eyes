# PLAYTEST-5l — slow touch vs fast flick (~5 min)

Doors now open two ways by hand:
- reach a hand slowly into the door -> the normal open (like pressing E / A)
- flick a hand fast into the door -> the slam open (the attack)

Start the game as usual, get into gameplay, then:
- open a few doors slowly, and a few with a fast flick
- find a hold-type interaction (e.g. a Gridnode) and keep your hand pressed on it until it finishes
- play normally for a couple of minutes
then quit normally.

1. Slow reach: normal door open?
2. Fast flick: door slam? If you got a different move (a punch/kick that doesn't slam), say so.
3. Speed: did slow reaches ever slam by mistake, or did real flicks sometimes only open normally?
4. Hold-type interaction (Gridnode etc.): finished while you kept the hand there?
5. Anything triggered that you didn't mean to?
6. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 fine, 4 yes, 5 no, 6 5".
