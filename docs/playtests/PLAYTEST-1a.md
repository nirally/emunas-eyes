# PLAYTEST-1a — First time in the headset: does looking around feel right? (~15 min)

**What to expect:** the game shows as a **flat picture, the same in both eyes** (no 3D yet, that's Stage 2), fixed in the world in front of you. It covers only part of your view, about the size of the game's field of view, with black around it. Looking around should feel **exactly 1:1**: the picture stays put in the world and you look around inside it. Turning and moving still use mouse and keyboard.

## Before you start
- **Nausea rule:** stop the moment you feel warm, sweaty or dizzy. Don't push through. Take off the headset and wait at least an hour. Tell me what you were doing; that gets logged as the top-priority bug.
- **Bug key: F11.** Press it the instant something feels wrong. It beeps and saves a report. You don't need to take the headset off.
- **Recentre: Numpad 5.** Do this whenever "straight ahead" is off.
- **Compare: Numpad 0** switches the head tracking off and on, so you can feel the difference.
- Optional: set the game's **Field of View to its maximum** in Options → Video for a bigger picture. The mod adapts automatically.

## Launch
1. Put on the Quest 2, open **Steam Link**, and connect to the PC so you're in SteamVR.
2. On the PC, start Mirror's Edge Catalyst from Steam. The game should appear in the headset after a few seconds.
3. How to quit: quit from the game's menu (or take off the headset and close it on the monitor).
