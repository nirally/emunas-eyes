# PLAYTEST-5i — gloved hands (~4 min)

The boxes are now dark gloved hands with a thin red band at the wrist. The fingers follow how you hold the controller: lift your index finger off the trigger to point, squeeze the grip to make a fist, lift your thumb off the stick/buttons to raise it.

Start the game as usual, get into gameplay, spend 2-3 minutes (look at your hands, point, make fists, then play a bit), then quit normally.

1. Do the hands sit where your real hands are? (or: too far forward/back/up/down, rotated)
2. Size: right, too big, or too small?
3. Fingers: does pointing, fist and thumb up/down follow you?
4. Look: OK, or anything odd (too dark/bright, blocky, flickering, hands showing through walls)?
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 a bit big, 3 yes, 4 ok, 5 5".
