# PLAYTEST-2f — anti-aliasing per eye (~10 min)

Why: the game's anti-aliasing blends each picture with the previous one. With one eye per frame, the previous picture was always the other eye's, so fine edges (railings, stairs, distant buildings) shimmered and the anti-aliasing never settled. Each eye now has its own. In my tests the picture is much steadier, but a little softer. Your eyes decide which is better.

Shift+F9 switches it on/off (F9 alone is still the full field of view). It starts ON.

In the headset (2880x1620):
1. Stand still and look at fine detail (railings, stairs, distant buildings). Press Shift+F9 a few times. Which is better: ON (steadier, softer) or OFF (sharper, shimmering)?
2. Same while running. Any smearing or ghost trails behind things with it ON?
3. Look down at Faith's body and hands with it ON: anything odd (ghosting, doubled edges)?
4. Anything new (stutter, flashes, wrong colours)?
5. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Leave it on whichever you preferred. Reply example: "1 on better, 2 no, 3 fine, 4 nothing, 5 5".
