# PLAYTEST-2g — Stage 2 sign-off: 3D picture (~15 min)

Why: this closes Stage 2 (the 3D picture). After it, Stage 3 starts: the Quest 2 controllers. Keep your current settings (2880x1620, everything on).

In the headset, play normally for ~10 minutes, then answer:
1. Close one eye, then the other, looking at shiny things (wet floors, glass, metal) and at shadows. Same in both eyes, apart from the small change of viewpoint? (yes/no + what)
2. Sky, sun or bright lights / lens flares: same in both eyes? (or "didn't see any")
3. Turn your head quickly: does anything pop in or vanish at the edges of your view?
4. Objects still feel the right size and depth? (1-5)
5. Carried from Stage 1: did you see a cutscene or do a takedown? Anything wrong there? (or "none")
6. Anything else wrong with the picture?
7. Comfort, 5 = very comfortable.

Reply example: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 no, 4 5, 5 none, 6 nothing, 7 5".
